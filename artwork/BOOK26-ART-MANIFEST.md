# Book 26 art manifest

Six journey plates for *The Censure of This World*. Five already exist in `public/assets/system/` (`book26-three-courtyards`, `book26-impermanence-court`, `book26-measured-provision`, `book26-civic-web`, `book26-balanced-road`). This manifest adds one new plate for journey 06. The app references its path already; dropping the files in makes it appear with no code change.

## Files

Alt text is already written into `src/book26.ts` and is what the plate has to depict. It is reproduced here as the brief. This book has six journeys; one plate is new.

**Set note.** Match the existing Book 26 plates: bright, clear Islamic architectural settings, no figures. The new plate is quieter than the courtyards: it belongs to the voices of the book (reports, sermons, and Uways by the Euphrates) rather than to its analysis.

### 6. `book26-the-warners.jpg` + `book26-the-warners-thumb.jpg`
- **Journey** 06 — What did those who knew the world say about it?
- **Accent colour** `#7a6aa8`
- **Must depict** A quiet riverbank at dawn with patched cloth drying on a flat stone, and beyond it a small mosque arch whose lamp is still burning.
- **Argument it carries** The journey ends with Uways al-Qarani, who washed the scraps of cloth he wore in the Euphrates and stitched them together, and who asked to stay unknown. The patched cloth on the stone is his whole possession; the lamp still burning at dawn is the night of worship and the sermons that close it. No person should appear: Uways wanted not to be found. Soft early light, still water, nothing costly in the frame.
