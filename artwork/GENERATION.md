## Shared direction

- Use case: scientific-educational
- Asset type: interactive reading concept plate
- Style: museum-quality isometric cut-paper and carved-stone educational illustration
- Format: landscape 3:2 with generous outer margin
- Light: soft museum daylight
- Palette: ivory limestone, charcoal, restrained emerald-blue inlay, and very small aged-gold details
- Constraints: no people, text, letters, Arabic, sacred inscriptions, logos, watermark, occult symbols, or fantasy glow

## Mirror obstructions

> Visualize al-Ghazali's heart-as-mirror analogy and the five reasons a mirror may fail to receive a clear form. Build one refined octagonal architectural display containing five visibly distinct mirror conditions: an unfinished mirror, a corroded mirror, a mirror turned away from its subject, a mirror blocked by a veil, and a polished mirror whose bearer does not know where to aim it. Use only physical staging to distinguish them. Keep all five conditions visually separate and accurate to the stated analogy. Avoid ornate clutter, modern UI, decorative calligraphy, and gradients.

## Reservoir pathways

> Visualize al-Ghazali's reservoir analogy for two ways knowledge reaches the heart. Show a single stone reservoir at the center of a precise cutaway landscape. On the upper surface, several clear streams flow into it from the surrounding terrain. In the cutaway below, earth is being cleared from beneath the reservoir to reveal a clean underground spring rising from within. The two routes must be instantly legible without text. Do not add a second reservoir or imply that one route cancels the other. Avoid ornate clutter, modern plumbing, decorative calligraphy, and gradients.

## Fortress entrances

> Visualize al-Ghazali's heart-as-fortress analogy for guarding the entrances through which destructive suggestions gain influence. Show an isometric octagonal stone fortress with a polished dark heart-shaped court at its protected center, multiple distinct gates and approach paths, a modest watch post, and visible thresholds that can be guarded. Outside the walls, represent recurring vulnerabilities only through restrained physical motifs near separate paths: a flame, an overflowing provision bowl, tangled coins, a laurel crown, a rushing wheel, and a fogged lens. Keep these symbolic objects small and non-mystical. Do not depict the unseen. Avoid battle scenes, horror, ornate clutter, and gradients.

> Preserve the existing educational composition, all conceptual stations, and the isometric viewpoint. Change the colour, daylight, materials, and planting so the plate feels luminous, hopeful, cultivated, and full of intellectual wonder. Use pearl white and sunlit ivory as the dominant field; generous warm architectural gold as structure and illumination; pale blue-grey and celadon coffers; restrained turquoise and lapis tile; living garden green; and small pomegranate, coral, saffron, and cobalt accents. Faithfully adapt the reference photograph's white carved stone panels, octagonal gold medallion rhythm, fine gold bands, pale geometric coffers, and warm lantern metalwork. Keep darkness localized only to an obstruction or destructive influence that requires it. No people, text, letters, Arabic, sacred inscriptions, logos, watermark, occult symbols, or fantasy glow. ### Plate-specific instructions

- **Inner city:** Preserve the central court, study, provisions room, watch post, workshops, sensory gates, and paths. Replace the black heart with polished warm golden amber, and cultivate the courts with flowering citrus or pomegranate trees.
- **Mirror obstructions:** Preserve the five distinct mirror conditions. Keep corrosion, veiling, incompletion, or misdirection localized to the relevant mirror; make the surrounding pavilion and central turquoise pool bright and restorative.
- **Reservoir pathways:** Preserve the one upper reservoir, surface streams, cutaway earth, and underground spring. Use clear turquoise water and visibly fertile planting with healthy trees, grasses, flowers, and fruiting shrubs.
- **Fortress entrances:** Preserve the gates, approach paths, watch post, and six small vulnerability motifs. Replace the black central heart with polished golden amber; keep fire, fog, and disorder small and localized outside the cultivated protected court.

## System journey illustrations

Four new symbolic journey plates were created with the built-in image generation tool on 2026-08-20. They are independent from the architectural plates above. Full-resolution PNG masters are preserved in `artwork/system-source/`; 1200-pixel JPEGs and 360-pixel navigation thumbnails are served from `public/assets/system/`.

### Shared prompt direction

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: web-app learning-journey illustration. Use warm ivory handmade paper, refined gouache, ink linework, mineral pigment, and restrained gold-leaf accents. Interpret historical manuscript craft through crisp contemporary editorial illustration. Compose a landscape 3:2 symbolic still life with clear edges and generous web-card margins. Use bright, hopeful illumination and a palette of warm ivory, lapis, turquoise, saffron, pomegranate coral, restrained metallic gold, and healthy botanical accents. Show no text, Arabic calligraphy, religious inscriptions, people, animals, anatomy, mosque architecture, angels, devils, unseen beings, logos, or watermarks. Avoid dark-green dominance, gloomy lighting, low saturation, ominous imagery, and fantasy effects.

### Journey-specific prompts

- **What is the heart?** Place a luminous brass compass-lantern at the centre, surrounded by four balanced orbital forms suggesting perception, reason, appetite, and protective force. Frame the outer edge with small flourishing botanical forms to suggest ordered life.
- **How does knowing happen?** Show a clear turquoise reservoir fed by two elegant channels beside a circular polished brass mirror catching a bright ray. Connect the water and mirror with fine diagram lines and frame them with fresh flowering plants.
- **How does a thought become an action?** Show one small luminous seed travelling through six increasingly defined brass-and-pigment circular gates, changing from a faint spark into a clear outward footprint-like geometric mark. Arrange the path with a strong left-to-right causal rhythm.
- **What makes change last?** Place a balanced brass compass at the centre of a flourishing garden. Circle it with two coloured currents in gentle tension and a pomegranate branch progressing from bud to fruit to suggest vigilance, support, and sustained growth.
