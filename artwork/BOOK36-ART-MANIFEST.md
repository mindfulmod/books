# Book 36 art manifest

Five journey plates for *Love, Longing, Intimacy, and Contentment*. The app references these paths already; dropping the files in makes them appear with no code change.

## Specs

| | |
|---|---|
| Serving directory | `public/assets/system/` |
| Master directory | `artwork/system-source/` (full-resolution PNG) |
| Full size | 1600 × 900 JPEG (16:9) |
| Thumbnail | 480 × 270 JPEG |
| Thumbnail path | derived automatically by replacing `.jpg` with `-thumb.jpg` — both files are required |
| Crop safety | the extreme top and bottom are cropped on narrow viewports; keep the load-bearing content out of those bands |

## Files

Alt text is already written into `src/book36.ts` and is what each plate has to depict. It is reproduced here as the brief.

### 1. `book36-the-sixth-sense.jpg` + `book36-the-sixth-sense-thumb.jpg`
- **Journey** 01 — What is love, actually?
- **Accent colour** `#278d91`
- **Must depict** A pale limestone sill holding five small vessels of scent, sound, fruit, cloth and light, with a sixth place set empty.
- **Argument it carries** Ghazali defines love as the nature's inclination toward what pleases it, then shows that one named beloved thing is reached by none of the five senses. The five vessels must read as a complete, orderly set; the sixth place must read as prepared and waiting, not as a gap or a loss. The emptiness is an argument, not an absence.

### 2. `book36-first-light.jpg` + `book36-first-light-thumb.jpg`
- **Journey** 02 — Why would this be the highest pleasure?
- **Accent colour** `#bf7a35`
- **Must depict** The same courtyard arch shown twice on one panel, once at first light and once at full noon, otherwise identical.
- **Argument it carries** The difference between knowing now and the sight promised is a difference of clarity along one line, not a difference of kind. The two halves must be provably the same arch — same stones, same angle, same shadow geometry — differing only in how much light has arrived. Any variation in the subject destroys the argument.

### 3. `book36-the-vessel.jpg` + `book36-the-vessel-thumb.jpg`
- **Journey** 03 — Why do people differ so much?
- **Accent colour** `#c25f50`
- **Must depict** A wide clay vessel half full of clear water on a scrubbed table, a dark flask waiting beside it.
- **Argument it carries** By the measure of what water remains, the vinegar poured in is diminished. This is displacement, not guilt: nothing in the frame should look spoiled, spilled, or reproachful. The water must be clean and the vessel sound. What the image says is only that the room is already taken.

### 4. `book36-three-views.jpg` + `book36-three-views-thumb.jpg`
- **Journey** 04 — Longing, intimacy, fear — which am I in?
- **Accent colour** `#586fa8`
- **Must depict** A single lamp on a stone ledge casting three distinct shadows on three walls of a bare room.
- **Argument it carries** Longing, intimacy, and fear are one love differing only by where the lover's gaze rests. There must be exactly one light source and it must be unmistakably single; the three shadows must be visibly the same object thrown three ways. If the image reads as three lamps, it argues the opposite of the chapter.

### 5. `book36-two-routes.jpg` + `book36-two-routes-thumb.jpg`
- **Journey** 05 — Can I be content with what I did not choose?
- **Accent colour** `#a97837`
- **Must depict** A worn stone threshold where two paths of inlaid tile meet, one pale and one dark, joining at a single door.
- **Argument it carries** Contentment is reached either by absorption, where the pain is not perceived, or by acceptance, where it is felt in full and the state stands alongside it. Both paths must arrive equally at the door and be equally worn; the dark path is not the lesser one. Ghazali gives the second route precisely so that feeling your affliction is not a disqualification.
