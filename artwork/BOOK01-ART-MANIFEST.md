## Files

Alt text is already written into `src/book01.ts` and is what each plate has to depict. It is reproduced here as the brief.

**Set note.** This book is a critique of scholars, and its images should stay cool and slightly wry rather than reverent. Four of the five are about things being empty, narrowed, or used up.

### 1. `book01-twenty-parties.jpg` + `book01-twenty-parties-thumb.jpg`
- **Journey** 01 — What am I actually obliged to know?
- **Accent colour** `#278d91`
- **Must depict** Four lecterns arranged in a circle facing inward, each holding a different open book, with an empty space at the centre.
- **Argument it carries** Every party placed the obligation on the knowledge it happened to be occupied with. The four lecterns must be equal in height and quality — no party is being ranked — and they must face *inward*, toward a centre where nothing stands. The empty centre is the question none of them answered.

### 2. `book01-the-cupper.jpg` + `book01-the-cupper-thumb.jpg`
- **Journey** 02 — What even counts as religious knowledge?
- **Accent colour** `#bf7a35`
- **Must depict** A craftsman's bench with a loom shuttle, a tailor's shears and a set of surgical cups laid out in an even row on clean linen.
- **Argument it carries** Ghazali classes weaving, tailoring, and cupping among the communal obligations of a town. The three tools must be laid out with the care usually given to books — even spacing, clean cloth, good light. The plate should read as a display of honoured instruments, because that is precisely the claim being made.

### 3. `book01-five-words.jpg` + `book01-five-words-thumb.jpg`
- **Journey** 03 — Why do the words not mean what they meant?
- **Accent colour** `#c25f50`
- **Must depict** Five brass nameplates on a plastered wall, four still bright and legible and the fifth rubbed smooth and blank.
- **Argument it carries** Five praised names were captured, and the last of them — wisdom — has widened until it selects nothing at all. The plates must be identical in size and mounting; only the fifth is worn blank. Do not letter the four with real words: the plate is about the mechanism, not about naming culprits.

### 4. `book01-the-cup.jpg` + `book01-the-cup-thumb.jpg`
- **Journey** 04 — What is wrong with arguing?
- **Accent colour** `#586fa8`
- **Must depict** A single overturned drinking cup on a low table, and beyond it an open doorway into a dark room.
- **Argument it carries** Disputation relates to the inward enormities as drink relates to the outward ones — the charge is about position, not magnitude. The cup must be small, plain, and unremarkable; the doorway beyond it is what carries the argument. Nothing in the room should be visible, and nothing should look violent.

### 5. `book01-the-wick.jpg` + `book01-the-wick-thumb.jpg`
- **Journey** 05 — What is all this learning for?
- **Accent colour** `#a97837`
- **Must depict** A single lamp burning on a broad table, its light reaching an open book, the wick visibly shortened.
- **Argument it carries** A wick that was kindled: it gives light to people while it burns. The light must genuinely reach the book and the page must be legible — the benefit to others is real, and that is what makes the image a warning rather than a dismissal. The shortened wick is the whole plate; keep it visible without making it the subject.

### `assets/system/book01-ten-duties.jpg` (+ `-thumb.jpg`)

**Journey 06 — "The student's ten duties"**
A stack of closed books on a plain desk beside a single sharpened reed pen.
Nothing open, nothing mid-use, no chair pulled out, no figure. Even side light.

Carries: the ten duties are about what has to be true *before* and *around* the
studying — character, attachments, a teacher, an order. The plate should read
as preparation, not as work in progress. If anything is open it reads as
studying and the point is lost.

Colour anchor: `#7a6ca8`.
