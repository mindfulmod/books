# Book 3 art manifest

Four journey plates for *The Mysteries of Purification*. The app references these paths already; dropping the files in makes them appear with no code change.

## Specs

| | |
|---|---|
| Serving directory | `public/assets/system/` |
| Master directory | `artwork/system-source/` (full-resolution PNG) |
| Full size | 1600 × 900 JPEG (16:9) |
| Thumbnail | 480 × 270 JPEG |
| Thumbnail path | derived automatically by replacing `.jpg` with `-thumb.jpg` — both files are required |
| Crop safety | the extreme top and bottom are cropped on narrow viewports; keep the load-bearing content out of those bands |

## Files

Alt text is already written into `src/book03.ts` and is what each plate has to depict. It is reproduced here as the brief. This book has four journeys rather than five.

**Set note.** Everything in this set should be scrupulously clean and plainly lit — the subject is cleanliness, and any grime, shadow, or texture-for-atmosphere works against it. Stone, water, and linen; no ornament.

### 1. `book03-four-ranks.jpg` + `book03-four-ranks-thumb.jpg`
- **Journey** 01 — Purity of what, exactly?
- **Accent colour** `#278d91`
- **Must depict** Four shallow stone basins set at rising heights on a plain wall, the lowest holding clear water and the highest empty and dry.
- **Argument it carries** Four ranks of purification: body, limbs, heart, inmost — the last named as the purity of the prophets and the truthful. The empty top basin is the plate's whole argument and must read as *clean and waiting*, not neglected. The basins must be identical in form and differ only in height, since they are one kind of thing at four levels.

### 2. `book03-declared-scope.jpg` + `book03-declared-scope-thumb.jpg`
- **Journey** 02 — Why is the rest of this book so technical?
- **Accent colour** `#bf7a35`
- **Must depict** A plain stone tablet leaning against a wall with a single horizontal line scored across its lower third.
- **Argument it carries** Ghazali declares that this book treats only the lowest of the four ranks, and says why. The scored line must be clean, deliberate, and unmistakably drawn by hand — a decision, not damage. The space above the line must be blank: what is excluded is treated elsewhere, not denied.

### 3. `book03-unchanged-water.jpg` + `book03-unchanged-water-thumb.jpg`
- **Journey** 03 — How strict does this actually have to be?
- **Accent colour** `#c25f50`
- **Must depict** A plain earthenware jar of clear water on a doorstep in strong daylight, entirely still and unattended.
- **Argument it carries** Four evidences converge on one observable test: whether the water has changed. The water must be visibly, obviously clear, and the jar plainly ordinary. *Unattended* is load-bearing — nobody is guarding it, and Umar performed ablution from a jar whose history he did not investigate. Nothing in the frame should suggest a question about what has touched it.

### 4. `book03-the-parings.jpg` + `book03-the-parings-thumb.jpg`
- **Journey** 04 — What happens to someone who perfects the outward?
- **Accent colour** `#586fa8`
- **Must depict** A washed pale bowl upturned on a cloth beside a small pair of shears and a folded towel, everything spotless.
- **Argument it carries** The easiest rank to perfect, given in the finest detail in the book — and capable of being done exactly while the third rank is untouched. Everything must be *immaculate*: this is a plate about something done perfectly. The mild unease should come only from how complete it looks, never from anything visibly wrong.
