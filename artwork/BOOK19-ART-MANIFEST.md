# Book 19 — Enjoining Right and Forbidding Wrong: art manifest

Three plates. Still objects, no figures, no faces, no crowds, no confrontation
scenes and nothing that reads as force or punishment. The book's own restraint
should be the register of the art.

## 1. `assets/system/book19-axis.jpg` (+ `-thumb.jpg`)

**Journey 01 — "The obligation, and who carries it"**
A single iron pivot pin standing upright in a worn stone socket, the stone
polished pale in a ring around it from long use. Close, low angle, raking light.
Nothing else in frame.

Carries: the greatest axis (al-qutb al-a'zam) — the thing everything else turns
on, and whose absence would leave the mechanism idle. The wear in the stone does
the work: it shows the turning has happened for a long time.

Colour anchor: `#c25f50`.

## 2. `assets/system/book19-wall.jpg` (+ `-thumb.jpg`)

**Journey 02 — "What may be addressed, and how you learned of it"**
A plain mudbrick wall with a closed wooden door, photographed straight on in
flat, even light. No gap, no keyhole emphasis, nothing visible past it.
Deliberately uninformative.

Carries: whoever shuts his door and screens himself with his walls. What passes
through is only a sound or a smell — and the image must give the viewer nothing,
because that is the point.

Colour anchor: `#278d91`.

## 3. `assets/system/book19-ladder.jpg` (+ `-thumb.jpg`)

**Journey 03 — "Ten rungs, and where yours stops"**
A wooden ladder leaning against a bare plastered wall, **its lowest rung missing**
— the two side rails empty where the first rung would be. Upper rungs intact and
receding into shadow at the top of the frame.

Carries: the ladder of ten degrees whose first rung is itself forbidden, and
whose upper rungs are closed where they are most tempting. The missing bottom
rung is the single most important detail in this manifest.

Colour anchor: `#bf7a35`.

## Constraints
- No human figures, no hands, no faces.
- No weapons, no implements of punishment, no restraints.
- No crowds, no confrontations, no authority figures or their insignia.
- No calligraphy, no text in frame.
- Thumbnails derived at render time from the `-thumb.jpg` suffix.
