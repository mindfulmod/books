# Book 20 — The Manners of Living and the Character of Prophecy: art manifest

Three plates. This book is a portrait of the Prophet, so the constraint is
absolute: **no figure, no face, no silhouette, no implied person anywhere in
frame** — not even a shadow or an empty chair that reads as a place someone
was. The plates are all about legibility and evidence, never about likeness.

## 1. `assets/system/book20-title.jpg` (+ `-thumb.jpg`)

**Journey 01 — "The outward as a title to the inward"**
A folded letter lying on a plain wooden table, its outer face inscribed (the
inscription illegible at this resolution — an even grey texture, not readable
script), the contents unseen and unopened. Flat overhead light, no hand, no
opener, nothing suggesting an imminent reader.

Carries: `'unwan` — the inscription on the outside of a letter that tells you
what is inside. The image works only if the viewer can see there IS an
inscription and cannot read it.

Colour anchor: `#278d91`.

## 2. `assets/system/book20-pressure.jpg` (+ `-thumb.jpg`)

**Journey 02 — "The tested registers"**
A bow hanging at rest on a bare wall — string slack, wood unbent, no arrow, no
quiver. Side-on, even light. It must read as *capacity not exercised*, which is
the whole point of the heading "pardon despite capability."

Carries: restraint that had every option open to it. If the bow reads as broken
or discarded rather than as slack, the plate fails.

Colour anchor: `#c25f50`.

## 3. `assets/system/book20-proof.jpg` (+ `-thumb.jpg`)

**Journey 03 — "The portrait as evidence"**
A wax seal impressed into a document, with the matrix that made it lying beside
it, face up. Both in frame, close, raking light so the relief reads. No
heraldry, no legible device — abstract relief only.

Carries: the closing argument — an impression legible enough to identify what
made it, and not producible by anything else. The matrix beside the seal is the
whole idea; do not crop it out.

Colour anchor: `#bf7a35`.

## Constraints
- **No human figure, face, silhouette, hand, or shadow of a person.**
- No depictions of, or allusions to, the Prophet in any form.
- No calligraphy and no legible text in frame — inscriptions must be texture.
- No weapons in use; the bow in plate 2 is at rest and unstrung of purpose.
