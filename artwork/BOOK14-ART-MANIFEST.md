# Book 14 art manifest

Three journey plates for *The Lawful and the Unlawful*. The app references these paths already; dropping the files in makes them appear with no code change.

## Specs

| | |
|---|---|
| Serving directory | `public/assets/system/` |
| Master directory | `artwork/system-source/` (full-resolution PNG) |
| Full size | 1600 × 900 JPEG (16:9) |
| Thumbnail | 480 × 270 JPEG |
| Thumbnail path | derived automatically by replacing `.jpg` with `-thumb.jpg` — both files are required |
| Crop safety | the extreme top and bottom are cropped on narrow viewports; keep the load-bearing content out of those bands |

## Files

Alt text is already written into `src/book14.ts` and is what each plate has to depict. It is reproduced here as the brief. This book has three journeys.

**Set note.** No people. This book's hardest argument is that investigating someone can itself be a wrong, so nothing here should invite the viewer to scrutinise a person. Keep every plate calm and unaccusing — the third in particular must not look like a scene of suspicion.

### 1. `book14-rain-in-air.jpg` + `book14-rain-in-air-thumb.jpg`
- **Journey** 01 — What counts as doubtful?
- **Accent colour** `#278d91`
- **Must depict** A wide shallow bowl held out in falling rain on open ground, with no roof or wall anywhere in the frame.
- **Argument it carries** The absolutely lawful is defined by an almost unreachable example: water taken from the rain before it has fallen on anyone's property. The absence of any structure is the whole plate — no roof, no wall, no boundary, nothing that could belong to anyone. Despite the alt text, no hands: prop or suspend the bowl so it reads as held out without a figure.

### 2. `book14-the-boundary.jpg` + `book14-the-boundary-thumb.jpg`
- **Journey** 02 — How careful should I be?
- **Accent colour** `#bf7a35`
- **Must depict** A low stone wall running across open pasture, with grazed grass on the near side and untouched grass beyond it.
- **Argument it carries** The shepherd grazing around the preserve has not trespassed and is likely to. The wall must be *low* — easily crossed, not a barrier — and the contrast between grazed and ungrazed grass must be visible right up to it. Nothing has gone wrong in this picture yet, and that is what it is about.

### 3. `book14-the-question.jpg` + `book14-the-question-thumb.jpg`
- **Journey** 03 — Should I ask where it came from?
- **Accent colour** `#c25f50`
- **Must depict** A covered dish set on a table before an empty chair, the cover not yet lifted.
- **Argument it carries** Asking is itself a harm — a tearing of a covering — and the scrupulous option is to decline gently rather than to investigate. The cover is doing double duty and must read as *hospitality*, not concealment: a good dish, well presented, offered to someone who has not yet sat down. Warm light. Nothing furtive.
