# Book 33 art manifest

Five journey plates for *Fear and Hope*. The app references these paths already; dropping the files in makes them appear with no code change.

## Specs

| | |
|---|---|
| Serving directory | `public/assets/system/` |
| Master directory | `artwork/system-source/` (full-resolution PNG) |
| Full size | 1600 × 900 JPEG (16:9) |
| Thumbnail | 480 × 270 JPEG |
| Thumbnail path | derived automatically by replacing `.jpg` with `-thumb.jpg` — both files are required |
| Crop safety | the extreme top and bottom are cropped on narrow viewports; keep the load-bearing content out of those bands |

## Files

Alt text is already written into `src/book33.ts` and is what each plate has to depict. It is reproduced here as the brief.

### 1. `book33-tillage.jpg` + `book33-tillage-thumb.jpg`
- **Journey** 01 — Is this hope or am I wishing?
- **Accent colour** `#278d91`
- **Must depict** A sunlit terraced field where one plot is tilled, watered, and cleared while an identical untended plot waits beside it.
- **Argument it carries** Expectation after the causes have been supplied is hope; the same expectation without them is wishing. The two plots must be identical in soil, light, and prospect, so that the only difference in the frame is the work. Neither plot should show a crop — the outcome is not the variable.

### 2. `book33-before-the-king.jpg` + `book33-before-the-king-thumb.jpg`
- **Journey** 02 — What is fear actually made of?
- **Accent colour** `#c25f50`
- **Must depict** A luminous audience hall where a single figure's empty place stands before a raised seat, with no intercessor's bench beside it.
- **Argument it carries** Fear is the pain of the heart at an expected harm, and it is proportioned to knowledge rather than to temperament. The absent intercessor's bench is the load-bearing detail: what makes the place fearful is what is known about the situation, not the atmosphere of the room. Keep the hall bright.

### 3. `book33-the-whip.jpg` + `book33-the-whip-thumb.jpg`
- **Journey** 03 — Is my fear doing anything?
- **Accent colour** `#586fa8`
- **Must depict** A bright stable court where a slender switch hangs beside a heavy harness, with a worn path leading out through an open arch.
- **Argument it carries** Fear is valued as a driver and not for itself; its measure is the movement it produces. The switch must be slight and the worn path unmistakable, so the image credits the travel rather than the instrument. Nothing in the frame should suggest cruelty or a cowering animal.

### 4. `book33-bread-and-water.jpg` + `book33-bread-and-water-thumb.jpg`
- **Journey** 04 — Which of the two do I need?
- **Accent colour** `#bf7a35`
- **Must depict** An ivory refectory sill where a loaf and a water jug stand side by side under even light, neither raised above the other.
- **Argument it carries** Neither fear nor hope is superior in itself; the question is which one this person needs now, as bread and water are not ranked but prescribed. The lighting must be scrupulously even and the two must occupy equal visual weight. Any hierarchy in the composition contradicts the chapter.

### 5. `book33-what-fills-it.jpg` + `book33-what-fills-it-thumb.jpg`
- **Journey** 05 — What are they all afraid of?
- **Accent colour** `#a97837`
- **Must depict** A quiet lamplit chamber at dusk where a single vessel stands filled to the brim, its contents unnamed and steady.
- **Argument it carries** The fear of the knowers converges on the ending rather than on any particular punishment. The vessel must be full and perfectly still, and what fills it must be genuinely unreadable — not water, not oil, not identifiable. The restraint is the point; naming the contents would name the fear.
