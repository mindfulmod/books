# Book 35 art manifest

Five journey plates for *Unity and Trust*. The app references these paths already; dropping the files in makes them appear with no code change.

## Specs

| | |
|---|---|
| Serving directory | `public/assets/system/` |
| Master directory | `artwork/system-source/` (full-resolution PNG) |
| Full size | 1600 × 900 JPEG (16:9) |
| Thumbnail | 480 × 270 JPEG |
| Thumbnail path | derived automatically by replacing `.jpg` with `-thumb.jpg` — both files are required |
| Crop safety | the extreme top and bottom are cropped on narrow viewports; keep the load-bearing content out of those bands |

## Files

Alt text is already written into `src/book35.ts` and is what each plate has to depict. It is reproduced here as the brief.

### 1. `book35-the-walnut.jpg` + `book35-the-walnut-thumb.jpg`
- **Journey** 01 — What do I actually believe?
- **Accent colour** `#278d91`
- **Must depict** A sunlit ivory sill holding one walnut opened in four stages, from whole husk to a single drop of oil.
- **Argument it carries** Unity has four degrees, and Ghazali's own image is a walnut with two shells, a kernel, and an oil within it. All four stages must be one walnut, shown progressively, not four different nuts. Each stage should look intact and deliberate: Ghazali says what each degree secures, and none of them is presented as waste.

### 2. `book35-the-advocate.jpg` + `book35-the-advocate-thumb.jpg`
- **Journey** 02 — Why am I still anxious?
- **Accent colour** `#586fa8`
- **Must depict** A bright court chamber where a sealed brief lies on an empty advocate's bench, the client's seat drawn back and still.
- **Argument it carries** Trust is derived from agency, and the client is at rest only if he believes four things of his advocate. The brief must be sealed and untouched and the client's seat empty — the plate shows the moment before reliance is tested. The room must be bright; the anxiety in this chapter is a belief, not an atmosphere.

### 3. `book35-three-stations.jpg` + `book35-three-stations-thumb.jpg`
- **Journey** 03 — Whose definition am I failing?
- **Accent colour** `#bf7a35`
- **Must depict** Three ivory alcoves in one wall: a writing desk with a brief, a low cradle, and an empty bier-cloth folded flat.
- **Argument it carries** The three stations of trust are the client with his advocate, the child with its mother, and the one in the hands of the washer. All three are real stations and the first is where most readers are, so the three alcoves must be equal in size and light. Nothing morbid: the third is a station, not a warning.

### 4. `book35-hand-and-heart.jpg` + `book35-hand-and-heart-thumb.jpg`
- **Journey** 04 — So do I work, or not?
- **Accent colour** `#c25f50`
- **Must depict** A luminous workshop sill where a tool lies mid-use beside a small brass weight resting perfectly level.
- **Argument it carries** The stirring of a hand is not the stirring of the inmost self, so working and relying are compatible. The tool must read as genuinely in use and the weight as genuinely undisturbed, in the same frame and the same light. Separating them into two zones would restore the opposition the chapter dissolves.

### 5. `book35-bounded-grant.jpg` + `book35-bounded-grant-thumb.jpg`
- **Journey** 05 — How far does this go?
- **Accent colour** `#a97837`
- **Must depict** An apothecary shelf where two sealed jars stand apart, one marked with a clear band and one with none, under even light.
- **Argument it carries** The permission to leave a remedy reaches only remedies whose effect is not established; Ghazali devotes a whole following section to refusing its generalisation. The band is the entire argument and must be unmistakable. The two jars must be otherwise identical, so the distinction lives in the marking and nowhere else.
