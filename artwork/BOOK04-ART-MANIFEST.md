# Book 4 art manifest

Five journey plates for *The Mysteries of Prayer*. The app references these paths already; dropping the files in makes them appear with no code change.

## Specs

| | |
|---|---|
| Serving directory | `public/assets/system/` |
| Master directory | `artwork/system-source/` (full-resolution PNG) |
| Full size | 1600 × 900 JPEG (16:9) |
| Thumbnail | 480 × 270 JPEG |
| Thumbnail path | derived automatically by replacing `.jpg` with `-thumb.jpg` — both files are required |
| Crop safety | the extreme top and bottom are cropped on narrow viewports; keep the load-bearing content out of those bands |

## Files

Alt text is already written into `src/book04.ts` and is what each plate has to depict. It is reproduced here as the brief.

**Set note.** No figures anywhere in this set, and nothing in a posture of prayer. The book's argument is about what is present in a heart, which no picture of a body can show — and a depicted worshipper would invite the exact reading Ghazali spends the third chapter refuting.

### 1. `book04-empty-room.jpg` + `book04-empty-room-thumb.jpg`
- **Journey** 01 — Is a distracted prayer a real prayer?
- **Accent colour** `#278d91`
- **Must depict** A bare prayer room with a single mat laid straight on the floor, swept clean and lit from one high window.
- **Argument it carries** Ghazali argues that presence of heart is a condition of the prayer, not an ornament of it. The room must be *perfectly* prepared and completely empty — everything outward in order, and the question of who is present left open. Nothing decorative on the walls; the single high window is the only source of light.

### 2. `book04-six-lamps.jpg` + `book04-six-lamps-thumb.jpg`
- **Journey** 02 — What is actually missing from my prayer?
- **Accent colour** `#bf7a35`
- **Must depict** Six identical lamps in a row along a stone shelf, four of them lit and two dark.
- **Argument it carries** Six inward meanings, separable, so that failure can be located rather than lamented. The lamps must be *identical* — these are six of one kind of thing — and the two unlit ones should be adjacent rather than scattered, so the eye reads a specific gap rather than general dimness. Which two are dark is the plate's whole content.

### 3. `book04-the-cloak.jpg` + `book04-the-cloak-thumb.jpg`
- **Journey** 03 — What actually fixes a wandering mind?
- **Accent colour** `#c25f50`
- **Must depict** A folded patterned cloak set aside on a bench with a plain undyed one laid out beside it, ready to be worn.
- **Argument it carries** The Messenger sent back a marked cloak saying it had just now distracted him, and asked for a plain one. The remedy is removal, not resistance. Both cloaks must look well-made and cared for — the patterned one is not being condemned, only set aside — and the plain one must be laid out ready, so the plate reads as a substitution and not a renunciation.

### 4. `book04-four-layers.jpg` + `book04-four-layers-thumb.jpg`
- **Journey** 04 — What should I be thinking about, exactly?
- **Accent colour** `#586fa8`
- **Must depict** A walnut set on a stone sill in four stages beside a folded cloth and a shallow basin of water.
- **Argument it carries** Place, clothes, skin, heart — container, covering, rind, kernel; Book 3's four ranks restated inside a single washing. The walnut deliberately echoes the Book 35 plate, and should: this is the same figure used for a different scheme. The cloth and basin must sit as equals beside it, since they are the outer two layers the image is about.

### 5. `book04-four-cycles.jpg` + `book04-four-cycles-thumb.jpg`
- **Journey** 05 — How does the rest of the book work?
- **Accent colour** `#a97837`
- **Must depict** Four concentric rings scored into a stone floor, the outermost broken by a single gap.
- **Argument it carries** Day, week, year, and occasion — four cycles covering every rhythm a life has. Three rings must be closed and unbroken; the outermost has one clean gap, which is the occasioned prayer that answers an event rather than a calendar. Without that gap the scheme is only a timetable, which is precisely what the fourth division prevents.
