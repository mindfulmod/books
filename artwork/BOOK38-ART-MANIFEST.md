# Book 38 art manifest

Five journey plates for *Vigilance and Self-Examination*. The app references these paths already; dropping the files in makes them appear with no code change.

## Specs

| | |
|---|---|
| Serving directory | `public/assets/system/` |
| Master directory | `artwork/system-source/` (full-resolution PNG) |
| Full size | 1600 × 900 JPEG (16:9) |
| Thumbnail | 480 × 270 JPEG |
| Thumbnail path | derived automatically by replacing `.jpg` with `-thumb.jpg` — both files are required |
| Crop safety | the extreme top and bottom are cropped on narrow viewports; keep the load-bearing content out of those bands |

## Files

Alt text is already written into `src/book38.ts` and is what each plate has to depict. It is reproduced here as the brief.

Plates 1 and 4 are a deliberate pair — the same counter at dawn and at dusk. They must share their camera angle, furniture, and props exactly, so that the only difference a viewer can name is the light and the state of the ledger.

### 1. `book38-the-ledger.jpg` + `book38-the-ledger-thumb.jpg`
- **Journey** 01 — Who am I actually dealing with?
- **Accent colour** `#278d91`
- **Must depict** A merchant's counter at dawn where an open ledger, a set of scales and a sealed purse are laid out untouched.
- **Argument it carries** The whole book is derived from a trading partnership: the intellect is the merchant, the soul the partner who disputes over the profit. The ledger must be open and blank, the scales empty and level, the purse sealed — nothing has been traded yet. The terms are set before anything happens, which is the first station.

### 2. `book38-the-shepherd.jpg` + `book38-the-shepherd-thumb.jpg`
- **Journey** 02 — What does watching actually mean?
- **Accent colour** `#bf7a35`
- **Must depict** A hillside at midday where a flock grazes in a close group with no fence in sight and one figure seated above them.
- **Argument it carries** Ghazali's image for the first degree of vigilance: the flock is set right by the shepherd, and the heart is the shepherd. The absence of any fence is the entire point — the flock must read as held together by attention rather than by enclosure. The seated figure should be still and unhurried, not herding.

### 3. `book38-first-link.jpg` + `book38-first-link-thumb.jpg`
- **Journey** 03 — Where do I stop it?
- **Accent colour** `#c25f50`
- **Must depict** A fine chain of five links laid out straight on pale stone, the first link open and the rest closed.
- **Argument it carries** A passing thought bequeaths desire, desire intent, intent purpose, purpose the act. The material of evil is cut at its first source. The open first link must read as easily parted and the four closed ones as progressively harder — this is a claim about leverage, not about guilt, so nothing in the frame should look broken or violent.

### 4. `book38-days-end.jpg` + `book38-days-end-thumb.jpg`
- **Journey** 04 — How do I actually audit a day?
- **Accent colour** `#586fa8`
- **Must depict** The same merchant's counter at dusk, the ledger now open at a written page, the scales holding two unequal weights.
- **Argument it carries** The pair to plate 1. Reckoning means looking at capital, profit, and loss separately, and charging what came out short. The written page and the unequal scales are the day's result; the purse from plate 1 should still be visible, because the capital is the quantity Ghazali says nobody counts.

### 5. `book38-three-souls.jpg` + `book38-three-souls-thumb.jpg`
- **Journey** 05 — What if it simply will not comply?
- **Accent colour** `#a97837`
- **Must depict** One lamp carried along a corridor, shown at three points on its path, the far end open and lit.
- **Argument it carries** The commanding soul, the reproachful soul, and the tranquil soul are three states of one soul, not three kinds of person, and reproach is the passage between them. It must be visibly the same lamp in all three positions, and the corridor must open at the far end — the destination is the tranquil soul, so nothing here should read as punishment or descent.
