# Book 18 — Listening and Ecstasy: art manifest

Three plates, matching the established treatment: still objects, no figures, no faces,
no musical instruments depicted in use, no gathering scenes. Restraint matters more
here than in any other book in the quarter — the subject is contested, and the art
must not read as endorsement or as ridicule.

## 1. `assets/system/book18-three-links.jpg` (+ `-thumb.jpg`)

**Journey 01 — "Meet the other side first"**
Three plain iron rings laid in a row on undyed cloth, touching but not joined —
a chain that has not been closed. Even lighting, shallow depth. The rings are
identical; nothing marks one as more important than another.

Carries: audition, ecstasy, movement as three separate links with three separate
questions. The fact that they touch but are not welded is the whole point.

Colour anchor: `#278d91`.

## 2. `assets/system/book18-one-sound.jpg` (+ `-thumb.jpg`)

**Journey 02 — "Take the four states"**
A single struck bell resting on the floor of an empty plastered room, with four
doorways opening off the room at equal distances. No figures in the doorways.
Light falls from a source outside the frame; the four openings are lit unequally.

Carries: one sound, four listeners, four different acts. The unequal light across
identical doorways carries the ranking without illustrating any of the states.

Colour anchor: `#bf7a35`.

## 3. `assets/system/book18-the-patches.jpg` (+ `-thumb.jpg`)

**Journey 03 — "The manners of the gathering"**
A length of plain undyed cloth cut into neat squares, the squares stacked in small
even piles on a bare wooden floor. The cut edges are clean. Soft, level light; nothing
torn, frayed or scattered.

Carries: Ghazali's ruling on tearing clothes. Ruining cloth by choice is waste; a robe
cut into squares useful as patches, and shared out, is allowed. The neatness of the
cuts is the whole point: the plate shows the permitted form, not the ecstatic one.

Colour anchor: `#c25f50`.

## Constraints
- No human figures, no hands, no faces.
- No instruments being played, no dancing, no gatherings.
- No calligraphy, no text in frame.
- Thumbnails derived at render time from the `-thumb.jpg` suffix.
