# Book 37 art manifest

Five journey plates for *Intention, Sincerity, and Truthfulness*. The app references these paths already; dropping the files in makes them appear with no code change.

## Specs

| | |
|---|---|
| Serving directory | `public/assets/system/` |
| Master directory | `artwork/system-source/` (full-resolution PNG) |
| Full size | 1600 × 900 JPEG (16:9) |
| Thumbnail | 480 × 270 JPEG |
| Thumbnail path | derived automatically by replacing `.jpg` with `-thumb.jpg` — both files are required |
| Crop safety | the extreme top and bottom are cropped on narrow viewports; keep the load-bearing content out of those bands |

## Files

Alt text is already written into `src/book37.ts` and is what each plate has to depict. It is reproduced here as the brief.

### 1. `book37-three-components.jpg` + `book37-three-components-thumb.jpg`
- **Journey** 01 — What is an intention, actually?
- **Accent colour** `#278d91`
- **Must depict** A pale workbench where a lamp, a key and a hand-tool lie in a row, arranged so that none can be used without the others.
- **Argument it carries** Every voluntary act needs knowledge, will, and power, and missing any one of them nothing happens. The three objects must read as a set with an evident order — the lamp shows, the key opens, the tool works — and the arrangement must make their mutual dependence visible. No object should dominate.

### 2. `book37-full-man.jpg` + `book37-full-man-thumb.jpg`
- **Journey** 02 — Can I just decide to mean it?
- **Accent colour** `#c25f50`
- **Must depict** A cleared table after a meal, the plate empty and pushed back, a second untouched dish set down beside it.
- **Argument it carries** An inclination cannot be invented by willing, as a full man cannot intend to have an appetite. The second dish must look genuinely appetising and the pushed-back plate must read as satisfaction rather than distaste. Nothing should suggest the food is at fault; the whole argument sits in the diner, not the dish.

### 3. `book37-pure-milk.jpg` + `book37-pure-milk-thumb.jpg`
- **Journey** 03 — What counts as sincere?
- **Accent colour** `#586fa8`
- **Must depict** A shallow white bowl of milk on a scrubbed stone counter, perfectly still and unclouded, in level daylight.
- **Argument it carries** Ghazali's own Quranic image: purity is the absence of anything that could mingle in. The milk must be flawlessly uniform and completely still, with no swirl, skin, or shadow crossing it. The plate must argue an absence, which means the frame should contain nothing that has been removed — no discarded jug, no strainer, no residue.

### 4. `book37-the-atom.jpg` + `book37-the-atom-thumb.jpg`
- **Journey** 04 — How mixed is too mixed?
- **Accent colour** `#bf7a35`
- **Must depict** A small brass balance on an ivory sill, its two pans holding almost equal weights, the beam very slightly tipped.
- **Argument it carries** Ghazali's rule is a proportion weighed to an atom, not a threshold: reward follows the excess of one motive over the other. The tip must be small enough that the viewer has to look to see which way it went — that difficulty is the chapter. A decisively tipped beam turns a scale back into a verdict.

### 5. `book37-six-meanings.jpg` + `book37-six-meanings-thumb.jpg`
- **Journey** 05 — What does truthfulness actually cover?
- **Accent colour** `#a97837`
- **Must depict** Six shallow niches cut into one plastered wall, each holding a plain unmarked token, lit evenly from the left.
- **Argument it carries** Truthfulness is used in six meanings, and one is called truthful relative to whichever of them he has a share in. The six niches must be one continuous wall rather than six separate frames, and the tokens must be unmarked — naming them would rank them, and Ghazali explicitly keeps partial truthfulness real.
