# Book 16 art manifest

Two journey plates for *The Etiquette of Seclusion*. The app references these paths already; dropping the files in makes them appear with no code change.

## Specs

| | |
|---|---|
| Serving directory | `public/assets/system/` |
| Master directory | `artwork/system-source/` (full-resolution PNG) |
| Full size | 1600 × 900 JPEG (16:9) |
| Thumbnail | 480 × 270 JPEG |
| Thumbnail path | derived automatically by replacing `.jpg` with `-thumb.jpg` — both files are required |
| Crop safety | the extreme top and bottom are cropped on narrow viewports; keep the load-bearing content out of those bands |

## Files

Alt text is already written into `src/book16.ts` and is what each plate has to depict. It is reproduced here as the brief. This is the shortest book of the quarter and has two journeys.

**Set note.** The book takes no side, so neither plate may either. Solitude must not look lonely and company must not look oppressive — the pair should read as two arrangements, both liveable, with a real cost either way.

### 1. `book16-one-lamp.jpg` + `book16-one-lamp-thumb.jpg`
- **Journey** 01 — What is actually gained by being alone?
- **Accent colour** `#278d91`
- **Must depict** A single lit window in a long wall of dark ones, seen from an empty street at night.
- **Argument it carries** Six benefits of withdrawal, from room for worship down to escaping tiresome company. The lit window must read as *chosen and occupied* — warm light, someone plainly awake and working — not as the last one left on. The dark windows are neighbours asleep, not an abandoned building. Nothing desolate.

### 2. `book16-two-doors.jpg` + `book16-two-doors-thumb.jpg`
- **Journey** 02 — What would I stop being able to do?
- **Accent colour** `#bf7a35`
- **Must depict** A study with a desk and books, its door standing open onto a courtyard where other doors face inward.
- **Argument it carries** Seven goods of company, each stated as a pair, so that half of each needs another person — and the ruling that one who has not yet learned what is obligatory is disobedient by seclusion. The study must be genuinely attractive and the courtyard genuinely inviting; the open door between them is the whole plate. Daylight, no figures.
