# Book 39 art manifest

Five journey plates for *Reflection*. The app references these paths already; dropping the files in makes them appear with no code change.

## Specs

| | |
|---|---|
| Serving directory | `public/assets/system/` |
| Master directory | `artwork/system-source/` (full-resolution PNG) |
| Full size | 1600 × 900 JPEG (16:9) |
| Thumbnail | 480 × 270 JPEG |

| Crop safety | the extreme top and bottom are cropped on narrow viewports; keep the load-bearing content out of those bands |

## Files

Alt text is already written into `src/book39.ts` and is what each plate has to depict. It is reproduced here as the brief.

This book's plates are the plainest set in the edition, and deliberately so: its argument is that reflection is an operation with parts rather than a contemplative mood, and that it should begin at what is nearest rather than at what is most impressive. Atmosphere would contradict the text.

### 1. `book39-two-knowledges.jpg` + `book39-two-knowledges-thumb.jpg`
- **Journey** 01 — What is reflection, actually?
- **Accent colour** `#278d91`
- **Must depict** Two shallow brass dishes on a pale sill, each holding a single seed, with a third empty dish set between and below them.
- **Argument it carries** Reflection is bringing two knowledges present in the heart so that a third is derived from them. The geometry must read as derivation and not as addition: the two full dishes above, the empty one below and between, so that the eye travels downward to what has not arrived yet. Two seeds, not a scattering — the premises are specific.

### 2. `book39-the-key.jpg` + `book39-the-key-thumb.jpg`
- **Journey** 02 — Why would thought come first?
- **Accent colour** `#bf7a35`
- **Must depict** A single iron key resting on a closed ledger at the end of a row of four plain doors, only the first ajar.
- **Argument it carries** Act follows state, state follows knowledge, knowledge follows thought — so thought is the beginning and the key. The four doors must be identical and in a line, and only the first open, so the sequence reads as ordered rather than ranked. The key is small and ordinary; the claim is about order, not about grandeur.

### 3. `book39-four-channels.jpg` + `book39-four-channels-thumb.jpg`
- **Journey** 03 — Where is thought allowed to run?
- **Accent colour** `#c25f50`
- **Must depict** A stone water-course splitting into four level channels of equal width, cut into pale flagstones.
- **Argument it carries** Ghazali claims the four channels are exhaustive: two concerning the servant and two concerning the Lord. All four must be the same width and the same depth, and there must be no fifth outlet and no overflow anywhere in the frame — the exhaustiveness is the claim, and any stray water contradicts it.

### 4. `book39-bat-and-sun.jpg` + `book39-bat-and-sun-thumb.jpg`
- **Journey** 04 — Why can I not think about that?
- **Accent colour** `#586fa8`
- **Must depict** A bright wall at midday with one small dark aperture, a slant of sunlight falling across the stone below it.
- **Argument it carries** The bat cannot bear the sun and goes about in what remains of its light where it has fallen on the earth. The restriction is about the eye and not about the light, so the sunlight must be plainly good — warm, clean, unthreatening — and the aperture must read as shelter rather than as menace. No bat should be visible; the plate is about the arrangement, not the creature.

### 5. `book39-nearest-thing.jpg` + `book39-nearest-thing-thumb.jpg`
- **Journey** 05 — So where do I actually start?
- **Accent colour** `#a97837`
- **Must depict** A single unremarkable pebble on a wide swept stone floor, lit plainly from above, with nothing else in the frame.
- **Argument it carries** Ghazali eliminates what has no known origin and what sight does not reach, and lands on what is nearest to understandings. The pebble must be genuinely ordinary — not beautiful, not unusual, not dramatically lit. Any attempt to make it striking defeats the plate, because the instruction is to start at the unimpressive thing in front of you.
